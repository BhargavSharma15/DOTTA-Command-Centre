Life Score Engine (30-Day Trajectory)

Your current trajectory score based on habit consistency over the last 30 days: (Checked ÷ Tracked) × 100. Earn your tier badge: S-Tier Titan (90-100%), A-Tier Executor (75-89%), B-Tier Average (50-74%), or Wake Up (<50%).

❄️

1-Day Streak Freeze Grace Period

Did not complete 100% of habits today? Your streak does not instantly reset to 0. You receive an automatic 1-day grace freeze (❄️). The streak only breaks if you fail 2 days in a row!

⏱️

Deep Work & Focus Hours

When any Pomodoro or study session reaches 00:00, your focus duration automatically logs into your weekly and monthly focus telemetry.

🔒

Locked In Metrics

A day is Locked In when all your habits are 100% completed. Track how many days you locked in this week (out of 7) and this month (out of 30).

Command Centre FAQs

How do the Modes work?

Use the top Dock to switch between Standard, Student, and Apex modes. Each mode provides a context-specific workspace, visual theme, and custom tools. Your data for all modes is saved securely.

How does the Habit Streak actually work?

The streak demands 100% execution. You must check every single habit box AND submit your Evening Debrief before midnight. Missing a habit resets your streak to 0.
